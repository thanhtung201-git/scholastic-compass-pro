# 📘 Users Guide

> **Module:** Users
> **Section:** Admin Settings
> **Access Levels:** Director, Super Admin

---

## 🌐 Overview
The **Users** menu handles the authentication and system access for all staff members. While the HR "Employees" module tracks personal and payroll info, this menu controls who can actually log into the application and what overarching permissions they possess.

## ✨ Key Features
- **Account Creation:** Invite new users and auto-generate their employee profiles simultaneously.
- **Status Management:** Toggle users between Active and Inactive to revoke login access instantly.
- **Bulk Imports:** Import massive lists of users via Excel when setting up a new branch.

---

## 🚀 How to Use (Step-by-Step)

### 1. Inviting a New User & Employee
- **Step 1:** Navigate to **Admin Settings > Users**.
- **Step 2:** Click the **Invite User** button to open the "Invite New User & Employee" dialog.
- **Step 3:** Fill out the comprehensive form fields:
  - **Full Name, Email, Phone Number:** Basic contact info.
  - **Role & Department:** Determine their system permissions (e.g., Teacher, Accountant).
  - **Branch:** Assign them to a specific campus.
  - **Contract Type & Base Salary:** For HR and Payroll synchronization.
  - **Start Date:** When they officially join.
- **Step 4:** Click **Invite User**. They will receive an email with login instructions.

### 2. Bulk Importing Users (Import Users)
- **Step 1:** Click the **Import Users (Nhân viên)** button.
- **Step 2:** Download the sample template.
- **Step 3:** Fill in the columns mapping to Tên (Name), Email, Chức vụ (Role), and Nhánh (Branch).
- **Step 4:** Upload the file. Review the preview table to spot any **Lỗi** (Errors) before confirming.

### 3. Managing Access
- **Step 1:** View the Users table. You will see columns for **Name, Role, Branch, Status, and Active**.
- **Step 2:** To revoke someone's login access (e.g., upon termination), toggle their **Active** switch off. This is safer than deleting the user, as it preserves their historical audit logs.

---

> [!TIP]
> **Pro Tip:** If a user forgets their password, you don't need to delete and recreate them. Ask them to use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page!
