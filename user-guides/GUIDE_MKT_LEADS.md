# 📘 Leads Guide\n\n> **Module:** Leads\n> **Section:** Marketing\n> **Access Levels:** Director, Admin, Marketing Manager, Marketing Staff\n\n---\n\n## 🌐 Overview\nManage potential customers, their contact info, and their progress through the sales funnel.\n\n## ✨ Key Features\n- Add new leads\n- Update lead status\n- View interaction history\n\n---\n\n## 🚀 Step-by-Step Instructions\n\n### Adding a Lead\n\n1. Go to **Leads** under Marketing.
2. Click **+ Add Lead**.
3. Fill in Name, Phone, Email, Source, etc.
4. Click **Save**.\n\n### Updating Status\n\n1. Open a lead.
2. Change the Status (e.g., New -> Contacted -> Enrolled).\n\n---\n*For further assistance, refer to the main User Guide or contact your System Administrator.*\n
