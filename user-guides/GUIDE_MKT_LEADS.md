# 📘 Leads Guide

> **Module:** Leads
> **Section:** Marketing
> **Access Levels:** Director, Admin, Marketing Manager, Marketing Staff

---

## 🌐 Overview
The **Leads** menu is the CRM core of your organization. A "Lead" is a prospective customer. This module allows your sales and marketing teams to capture contact details, assign them to specific staff members, and track them until they either enroll or decline.

## ✨ Key Features
- **Lead Capture:** Record extensive details including name, company, email, and the specific marketing Source.
- **Staff Assignment:** Delegate leads to specific sales representatives for follow-up.
- **Detailed Tracking:** Maintain a central table showing the last contact date and current conversion status.

---

## 🚀 How to Use (Step-by-Step)

### 1. Adding a New Lead
- **Step 1:** Navigate to **Marketing > Leads**.
- **Step 2:** Click the button to open the **Add New Lead** dialog.
- **Step 3:** Fill in the contact form:
  - **Full Name *:** The lead's primary identifier.
  - **Lead Type, Company Name, Job Title:** Helpful for corporate or B2B sales.
  - **Email, Phone, Address:** Standard contact info.
  - **Source:** Where did they come from? (e.g., Facebook Ads, Word of Mouth).
  - **Assigned Staff:** Choose which of your team members is responsible for calling them.
  - **Notes:** Any initial requests the lead made.
  - **Status:** Usually defaults to "New" or "Pending".
- **Step 4:** Click Save.

### 2. Managing the Lead Pipeline
- **Step 1:** The main dashboard displays a comprehensive table with columns: **Full Name, Type, Company, Phone, Email, Source, Assigned To, Status, Last Contact, Created, and Actions**.
- **Step 2:** Use the **Edit Lead** action to update their Status as they progress through your sales funnel (e.g., changing from "New" to "Contacted" to "Enrolled").
- **Step 3:** You can also use the Action menus to open sub-dialogs like **Add Activity**, **Add Note**, or **Add Task** to log exactly what happened during your last phone call with them.

---

> [!TIP]
> **Pro Tip:** Keep a close eye on the "Last Contact" column. If a lead hasn't been contacted in over 7 days, you should immediately assign a Task to your staff to reach out again before the lead goes cold!
