# 📘 Payroll Guide

> **Module:** Payroll
> **Section:** Finance & Accounting
> **Access Levels:** Director, Admin, Finance Manager, Accountant

---

## 🌐 Overview
The **Payroll** menu is used by the finance team to process and finalize wage payments for teachers and staff. It automatically calculates total pay based on scheduled hours and configured hourly rates.

## ✨ Key Features
- **Payslip Generation:** Automatically compile base salary, bonuses, and deductions into a single payslip.
- **Approval Workflow:** Review and approve timesheets before officially recording the payroll expense.
- **Detailed Breakdowns:** Review the specific classes, dates, and hours that make up a teacher's total pay.

---

## 🚀 How to Use (Step-by-Step)

### 1. Generating a New Payslip
- **Step 1:** Navigate to **Finance & Accounting > Payroll**.
- **Step 2:** Click the **Generate Payslip** button to open the generation dialog.
- **Step 3:** Fill in the form fields:
  - **Teacher *:** Select the employee you are paying.
  - **Period (Month) *:** Select the specific month this payment covers.
  - **Bonus (VND):** Add any extra performance bonuses.
  - **Deductions (VND):** Subtract any taxes or penalties.
- **Step 4:** Click the **Generate Payslip** action to finalize the calculation.

### 2. Reviewing and Approving Payroll
- **Step 1:** The main table displays columns for **Teacher, Period, Base Salary, Bonus, Deductions, Total, and Status**.
- **Step 2:** Click on an entry to see the deeper breakdown columns: **Class, Date, Hours, Rate, and Total Pay** for that specific period.
- **Step 3:** If everything looks accurate, click the **Approve** button to mark the payslip as finalized and officially paid.

---

> [!WARNING]  
> **Warning:** Once you click "Approve" on a payslip, the total amount is permanently recorded as an expense in your Balance Sheet. Ensure all numbers are correct before approving.
