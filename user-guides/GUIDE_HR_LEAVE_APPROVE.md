# 📘 Leave Approve Guide

> **Module:** Leave Approve
> **Section:** Human Resources
> **Access Levels:** Director, Admin, HR Manager, HR Staff

---

## 🌐 Overview
The **Leave Approve** menu acts as the central inbox for all staff time-off requests. It ensures that managers can review requests, see why time off is needed, and officially approve or reject them so that the Payroll system stays accurate.

## ✨ Key Features
- **Centralized Inbox:** See all pending leave requests across the entire organization.
- **One-Click Approvals:** Quickly confirm requests with a single button.
- **Payroll Integration:** Approved leave automatically syncs so employees are not penalized for authorized absences.

---

## 🚀 How to Use (Step-by-Step)

### 1. Reviewing Pending Requests
- **Step 1:** Navigate to **Human Resources > Leave Approve**.
- **Step 2:** Review the main dashboard table. You will see requests broken down by: **Nhân sự (Staff), Phòng ban (Department), Lý do (Reason), Thời gian (Dates), Số ngày (Total Days), Trạng thái (Status), and Hành động quản lý (Management Actions)**.
- **Step 3:** Pay special attention to the "Lý do" column to determine if the leave is justified.

### 2. Approving or Rejecting Leave
- **Step 1:** Locate a request where the "Trạng thái" is marked as Pending.
- **Step 2:** Under the "Hành động quản lý" column, click the **Xác nhận** (Confirm) button.
- **Step 3:** The request will be officially approved. The employee's attendance record will automatically reflect their approved absence for those specific dates.

---

> [!TIP]
> **Pro Tip:** Always check the "Phòng ban" (Department) column before hitting confirm! If three people from the same department all request the same day off, you might leave that department dangerously understaffed.
