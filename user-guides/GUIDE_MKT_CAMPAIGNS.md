# 📘 Campaigns Guide

> **Module:** Campaigns
> **Section:** Marketing
> **Access Levels:** Director, Admin, Marketing Manager, Marketing Staff

---

## 🌐 Overview
The **Campaigns** menu tracks your large-scale marketing initiatives—such as a "Summer Discount Promo" or "Facebook Advertising Push". By defining campaigns, you can measure exactly how much money was spent and compare it to the number of leads generated.

## ✨ Key Features
- **Campaign Creation:** Define timelines, budgets, and the primary advertising channel.
- **Status Tracking:** Easily toggle campaigns between planning, active, and completed.
- **Performance Overview:** A dedicated table lets you review budgets and timelines at a glance.

---

## 🚀 How to Use (Step-by-Step)

### 1. Creating a New Campaign
- **Step 1:** Navigate to **Marketing > Campaigns**.
- **Step 2:** Click the button to open the **Add New Campaign** dialog.
- **Step 3:** Fill in the mandatory form fields:
  - **Campaign Name *:** E.g., "Back to School 2026".
  - **Channel *:** Select the primary medium (e.g., Social Media, Print).
  - **Start Date * & End Date:** Set the active timeline.
  - **Budget:** How much money is allocated.
  - **Description:** Outline the goals or target audience.
  - **Status:** Set to "Active" if it's currently running.
- **Step 4:** Save the campaign.

### 2. Managing Campaigns
- **Step 1:** Review the main dashboard table. You will see columns for **Campaign Name, Channel, Budget, Start Date, End Date, Status, and Actions**.
- **Step 2:** Use the **Actions** menu to open the **Edit Campaign** dialog if you need to increase the budget or extend the End Date.
- **Step 3:** Ensure you update the Status to "Completed" when the campaign finishes so your active view stays clean.

---

> [!TIP]
> **Pro Tip:** When a new lead calls in, ask them "Where did you hear about us?" and make sure your staff tags the lead with the correct Campaign Name in the Leads menu. This is the only way to prove if your marketing budget was well spent!
