# 📘 Dashboard Guide

> **Module:** Dashboard
> **Section:** Core
> **Access Levels:** All Roles (Views vary by permission)

---

## 🌐 Overview
The **Dashboard** is the first screen you see when you log into the ERP. It acts as the mission control center for your daily operations, providing an at-a-glance summary of your most critical metrics, pending tasks, and recent alerts.

## ✨ Key Features
- **High-Level Metrics:** See total active students, monthly revenue, and pending tasks immediately.
- **Quick Links:** Jump directly to urgent items (like overdue invoices or pending leave approvals) without navigating through multiple menus.
- **Role-Based Views:** A teacher's dashboard will highlight their daily schedule, while a finance manager's dashboard will highlight cash flow.

---

## 🚀 How to Use (Step-by-Step)

### 1. Reviewing Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- **Step 1:** Navigate to **Core > Dashboard** (or simply click the logo in the top left corner).
- **Step 2:** Review the summary cards at the top of the screen. These cards display aggregate numbers (e.g., "Total Students: 450", "Pending Leads: 12").
- **Step 3:** Compare these numbers against historical averages to get a quick pulse on the business's health.

### 2. Addressing Urgent Action Items
- **Step 1:** Scroll down to view the operational widgets (e.g., "Recent Tasks", "Upcoming Classes", or "Pending Approvals").
- **Step 2:** Click directly on an item within these widgets (like a pending leave request) to be transported straight to the relevant menu to resolve it.

---

> [!TIP]
> **Pro Tip:** Make checking the Dashboard your first habit every morning. It is specifically designed to highlight the things that are broken, pending, or overdue so you know exactly what to prioritize for the day.
