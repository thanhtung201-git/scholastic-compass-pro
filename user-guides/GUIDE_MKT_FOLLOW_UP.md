# 📘 Follow-up Guide

> **Module:** Follow-up
> **Section:** Marketing
> **Access Levels:** Director, Admin, Marketing Manager, Marketing Staff

---

## 🌐 Overview
The **Follow-up** menu acts as the CRM activity log for your marketing team. It tracks all communication touchpoints with your Leads, ensuring that no potential student is forgotten and providing a historical record of what was discussed.

## ✨ Key Features
- **Activity Tracking:** Log calls, emails, and in-person meetings.
- **Scheduling:** Set deadlines and prioritize upcoming follow-ups.
- **Comprehensive Dashboards:** See a list of all your scheduled outreach tasks in one place.

---

## 🚀 How to Use (Step-by-Step)

### 1. Creating a Follow-up Task
- **Step 1:** Navigate to **Marketing > Follow-up**.
- **Step 2:** Click to open the **Add Follow-up** dialog.
- **Step 3:** Fill out the outreach details:
  - **Lead *:** Select the prospective student this relates to.
  - **Assigned Staff *:** Assign this task to yourself or a team member.
  - **Type *:** Was it a Call, Email, or Meeting?
  - **Title & Note:** Summarize the discussion (e.g., "Parent wants to wait until September").
  - **Deadline * & Priority:** When does the *next* action need to happen?
  - **Status:** Is it "Pending" or "Completed"?
- **Step 4:** Save the follow-up log.

### 2. Managing Your Pipeline
- **Step 1:** Check the main table daily. It displays columns for: **Lead, Assigned Staff, Type, Title, Deadline, Priority, Status, and Actions**.
- **Step 2:** Sort by "Deadline" to see who you need to call today.
- **Step 3:** After making a call, use the **Edit Follow-up** action to update its status to "Completed" and immediately create a new follow-up for the next step.

---

> [!TIP]
> **Pro Tip:** Never leave a lead "hanging". Every time you finish a follow-up call and mark it "Completed," immediately schedule the *next* follow-up date, even if it's 3 months away!
