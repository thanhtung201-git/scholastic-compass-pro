# 📘 Sources Guide

> **Module:** Sources
> **Section:** Marketing
> **Access Levels:** Director, Admin, Marketing Manager

---

## 🌐 Overview
The **Sources** menu allows administrators to define the exact channels from which potential students originate (e.g., Facebook Ads, Word of Mouth, Walk-ins). By maintaining a clean list of sources, your marketing reports can accurately identify which platforms yield the highest return on investment.

## ✨ Key Features
- **Centralized Source Definition:** Create specific tags that will appear as dropdown options in the Leads menu.
- **Status Toggling:** Deactivate old or ineffective sources without deleting historical data.

---

## 🚀 How to Use (Step-by-Step)

### 1. Adding a New Lead Source
- **Step 1:** Navigate to **Marketing > Sources**.
- **Step 2:** Click the button to open the **Add New Lead Source** dialog.
- **Step 3:** Fill in the configuration fields:
  - **Source Name *:** Make it short and recognizable (e.g., "Google Search").
  - **Description:** Optional context (e.g., "Organic traffic from our website SEO").
  - **Active:** Ensure this is toggled on so it appears in dropdowns.
- **Step 4:** Click to Save.

### 2. Managing Existing Sources
- **Step 1:** Review the main table under columns: **Source Name, Description, Status, Created, and Actions**.
- **Step 2:** If you stop using a specific advertising platform, click **Actions** and select the **Edit Lead Source** option.
- **Step 3:** Toggle the "Active" status to off. This removes it from future Lead forms but preserves it on all past records!

---

> [!WARNING]  
> **Warning:** Be incredibly careful if you choose to "Delete" an action rather than just setting it to inactive. Deleting a source that is already attached to hundreds of past leads could corrupt your historical reporting.
