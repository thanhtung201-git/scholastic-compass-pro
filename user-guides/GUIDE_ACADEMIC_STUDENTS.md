# 📘 Students Guide

> **Module:** Students
> **Section:** Academic
> **Access Levels:** Director, Admin, Academic Manager, Academic Staff

---

## 🌐 Overview
The **Students** menu manages the master list of all enrolled learners. It handles their contact info, parent details, class enrollments, and provides quick insights into their tuition payment status.

## ✨ Key Features
- **Comprehensive Profiles:** Store names, emails, and parent contacts in one place.
- **Quick Status Tracking:** See if a student is "Đang học" (Studying), "Bỏ học" (Dropped), or "Đã hoàn thành" (Completed).
- **Automated Workflows:** Adding a student here automatically creates their class enrolment and generates their first Tuition Invoice!

---

## 🚀 How to Use (Step-by-Step)

### 1. Adding a New Student
- **Step 1:** Navigate to **Academic > Students**.
- **Step 2:** Click the **Add Student** button to open the "Add New Student" modal.
- **Step 3:** Fill out the mandatory form fields:
  - **Full Name, Email, Phone:** The student's primary contact details.
  - **Parent's Name:** Crucial for younger students.
  - **Enrolled Class:** Select which active class they are joining.
  - **Branch:** Select the campus they attend.
- **Step 4:** Click **Add Student**. The system will handle the background creation of their enrolment records.

### 2. Filtering and Searching the Roster
- **Step 1:** Use the table columns (**Student, Contact, Class, Branch, Status, Last Payment, Tuition**) to review the roster.
- **Step 2:** If you have hundreds of students, use the filter dropdowns at the top of the page.
- **Step 3:** Select specific Branches, Classes, or Statuses to narrow down the view. Click **Clear All** to reset your filters.

### 3. Editing Student Profiles
- **Step 1:** Click the Edit icon next to a student in the table to open the "Edit Student" dialog.
- **Step 2:** You can update their contact info or change their **Enrolled Class** if they are transferring.

---

> [!TIP]
> **Pro Tip:** Keep an eye on the "Tuition" column in the table view! It highlights in red if their latest invoice is "Unpaid", making it very easy for reception staff to know who needs a payment reminder when they walk through the door.
