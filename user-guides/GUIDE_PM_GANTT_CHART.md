# 📘 Gantt Chart Guide

> **Module:** Gantt Chart
> **Section:** Project Management
> **Access Levels:** All Roles

---

## 🌐 Overview
The **Gantt Chart** provides a powerful visual timeline of your complex projects. By displaying tasks horizontally across a calendar grid, it allows managers to immediately spot overlapping schedules, tight deadlines, and workflow bottlenecks.

## ✨ Key Features
- **Timeline Visualization:** See the chronological flow of project tasks over weeks or months.
- **Dependency Tracking:** Visually understand which tasks must finish before others can begin.
- **Schedule Management:** Easily identify if a project is running behind schedule.

---

## 🚀 How to Use (Step-by-Step)

### 1. Viewing Project Timelines
- **Step 1:** Navigate to **Project Management > Gantt Chart**.
- **Step 2:** Use the dropdown filter at the top of the page to select a specific **Project** you wish to analyze.
- **Step 3:** The timeline will populate with horizontal bars. 
  - Each bar represents a specific task. 
  - The left edge of the bar represents the task's Start Date.
  - The right edge represents the task's Due Date.

### 2. Identifying Risk
- **Step 1:** Scan the chart vertically. If you see multiple bars stacked on top of each other in the same column, it means your team is scheduled to work on several tasks simultaneously.
- **Step 2:** If the stack is too high, your team might be overloaded for that specific week, putting the project timeline at risk.
- **Step 3:** Navigate to the **Task Assignment** menu to push back due dates or reassign tasks to alleviate the pressure.

---

> [!TIP]
> **Pro Tip:** Use the Gantt Chart during your weekly team meetings. It is the absolute best way to visually communicate the "critical path" to your staff so they understand *why* certain deadlines cannot be missed.
