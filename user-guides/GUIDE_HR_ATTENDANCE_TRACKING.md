# 📘 Attendance Tracking Guide

> **Module:** Attendance Tracking
> **Section:** Human Resources
> **Access Levels:** Director, Admin, HR Manager, HR Staff

---

## 🌐 Overview
The **Attendance Tracking** menu is used by HR to monitor the daily check-ins and check-outs of all staff. Accurate logs are critical because they feed directly into the Payroll module.

## ✨ Key Features
- **Daily Overviews:** See who has arrived, who is late, and who is absent at a glance.
- **Manual Adjustments:** Allow HR to manually check staff in or out if they forget to swipe their badges.

---

## 🚀 How to Use (Step-by-Step)

### 1. Reviewing Daily Attendance
- **Step 1:** Navigate to **Human Resources > Attendance Tracking**.
- **Step 2:** The dashboard displays a large data table with the following columns: **Giáo viên / Cán bộ (Staff), Ngày điểm danh (Date), Nút check-in (Check-in time), Nút check-out (Check-out time), Trạng thái (Status), and Ghi chú (Notes)**.
- **Step 3:** Look for anomalies in the "Trạng thái" column, such as staff marked as "Late" or "Absent".

### 2. Making Manual Adjustments
- **Step 1:** If a staff member forgot to check out at the end of the day, you will see a blank space in the "Nút check-out" column.
- **Step 2:** Click the respective check-in or check-out button to manually log their time.
- **Step 3:** Be sure to add a brief explanation in the **Ghi chú** (Notes) column so the Finance team knows why a manual adjustment was made prior to running payroll.

---

> [!WARNING]  
> **Warning:** Do not make manual adjustments to dates that have already been processed in the Payroll module. Changing past attendance records will cause discrepancies in your financial audits.
