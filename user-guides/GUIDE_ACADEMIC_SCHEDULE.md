# 📘 Schedule Guide

> **Module:** Schedule
> **Section:** Academic
> **Access Levels:** Director, Admin, Academic Manager, Academic Staff

---

## 🌐 Overview
The **Schedule** menu is the master calendar for all academic operations. It provides a visual timeline of when and where classes are taking place, helping managers avoid room conflicts and ensuring teachers know exactly when they are required to be on campus.

## ✨ Key Features
- **Visual Calendar:** Navigate week-by-week to see the layout of all classes.
- **Lesson Scheduling:** Add specific lessons, linking a class to a room, teacher, and timeframe.
- **Conflict Management:** Easily view the grid to ensure two classes aren't assigned to the same room at the same time.

---

## 🚀 How to Use (Step-by-Step)

### 1. Navigating the Calendar
- **Step 1:** Navigate to **Academic > Schedule**.
- **Step 2:** The calendar defaults to the current week. Use the **<** and **>** arrow buttons to move backward or forward by 7 days.
- **Step 3:** Click the **Today** button to instantly jump back to the current date.

### 2. Adding a Lesson to the Schedule
- **Step 1:** Click the **Add Lesson** button to open the **Add Lesson to Schedule** dialog.
- **Step 2:** Fill out the scheduling details:
  - **Class:** Select the cohort (e.g., IELTS-A03).
  - **Date:** The specific day the lesson occurs.
  - **Start / End:** The exact times (e.g., 09:00 to 11:00).
  - **Teacher & Room:** Verify the assigned instructor and physical location.
- **Step 3:** Click **Save Changes** (or Add).

### 3. Editing an Existing Lesson
- **Step 1:** Click directly on any scheduled block in the calendar view.
- **Step 2:** The **Edit Lesson** dialog will appear.
- **Step 3:** Modify the times, teacher, or room (e.g., if you need to arrange a substitute teacher at the last minute), then save.

---

> [!TIP]
> **Pro Tip:** If a teacher is running late or absent, use the Edit Lesson modal to quickly swap in a substitute teacher so the payroll system accurately captures the hours worked by the substitute.
