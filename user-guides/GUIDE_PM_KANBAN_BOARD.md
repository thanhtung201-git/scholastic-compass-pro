# 📘 Kanban Board Guide

> **Module:** Kanban Board
> **Section:** Project Management
> **Access Levels:** All Roles

---

## 🌐 Overview
The **Kanban Board** provides a highly visual, drag-and-drop interface for managing your team's tasks. It takes all the items from the "Task Assignment" menu and displays them as interactive cards across different workflow stages.

## ✨ Key Features
- **Drag & Drop Workflow:** Effortlessly move tasks from "Todo" to "In Progress" to "Done".
- **Visual Accountability:** See instantly what everyone on the team is currently working on.
- **Quick Task Creation:** Add new tasks directly to the board without switching menus.

---

## 🚀 How to Use (Step-by-Step)

### 1. Moving Tasks Through Stages
- **Step 1:** Navigate to **Project Management > Kanban Board**.
- **Step 2:** Locate your assigned task card in the **Todo** column.
- **Step 3:** Click and hold the card, then drag it into the **In Progress** column to indicate you have started working on it.
- **Step 4:** Once finished, drag it to the **Done** column. The underlying database instantly updates the task's status.

### 2. Creating Tasks Directly on the Board
- **Step 1:** Click the **New Task** button located near the top of the board to open the **Create New Task** dialog.
- **Step 2:** Fill in the title, description, assignee, and priority.
- **Step 3:** Click **Create**. The task will immediately populate as a new card in the "Todo" column.

---

> [!TIP]
> **Pro Tip:** If you notice a massive pileup of cards in the "Review" or "In Progress" columns, that indicates a bottleneck! You should jump into the "Workload View" menu to rebalance tasks to team members who have fewer cards assigned to them.
