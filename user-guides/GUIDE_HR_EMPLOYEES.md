# 📘 Employees Guide

> **Module:** Employees
> **Section:** Human Resources
> **Access Levels:** Director, Admin, HR Manager, HR Staff

---

## 🌐 Overview
The **Employees** menu provides HR with a unified directory of all administrative staff. It gives a quick overview of contact information, roles, and financial standing for every non-teaching employee.

## ✨ Key Features
- **Staff Directory:** A searchable list mapping employees to their specific roles and departments.
- **Payroll Quick-Glance:** See an employee's total salary earnings at a glance.

---

## 🚀 How to Use (Step-by-Step)

### 1. Browsing the Employee Directory
- **Step 1:** Navigate to **Human Resources > Employees**.
- **Step 2:** You will be presented with the main directory table featuring the following columns: **Full Name, Role, Department, Phone, Total Salary, and Detail**.
- **Step 3:** Use this list to quickly find contact numbers or verify which department an employee belongs to.

### 2. Viewing Employee Details
- **Step 1:** Locate the specific employee you need to investigate.
- **Step 2:** Look at the **Total Salary** column for a quick financial summary.
- **Step 3:** Click the **Detail** action to dive deeper into their individual HR profile (such as their historical attendance records, leave requests, and specific contract terms).

---

> [!TIP]
> **Pro Tip:** Teaching staff are handled separately because they have different hourly pay structures and class schedules. If you are looking for an instructor, check the **Teachers** menu instead!
