# 📘 Salary Guide

> **Module:** Salary
> **Section:** Finance & Accounting
> **Access Levels:** Director, Admin, Finance Manager

---

## 🌐 Overview
The **Salary** menu is the configuration engine for your payroll system. It is where you define the foundational compensation structures for all employees, determining how much they earn before actual payslips are generated.

## ✨ Key Features
- **Compensation Profiles:** Define fixed monthly salaries for administrative staff or hourly rates for teachers.
- **Contract Tracking:** Keep track of contract types (e.g., Full-time, Part-time).
- **Centralized Adjustment:** Updating a base salary here ensures all future payroll runs use the correct rate automatically.

---

## 🚀 How to Use (Step-by-Step)

### 1. Setting Up Employee Salaries
- **Step 1:** Navigate to **Finance & Accounting > Salary**.
- **Step 2:** Select an employee from the directory list.
- **Step 3:** Open their compensation profile by clicking the **Edit** or **Update Salary** action.
- **Step 4:** Fill out the specific compensation fields:
  - **Contract Type:** Specify if they are permanent, temporary, or hourly.
  - **Base Salary / Hourly Rate:** Enter the numerical value in VND.
  - **Allowances:** If applicable, note standard monthly allowances (e.g., travel or meal stipends).
- **Step 5:** Save the configuration.

### 2. Processing Rate Increases
- **Step 1:** When an employee earns a promotion or raise, locate their profile in the Salary menu.
- **Step 2:** Update their **Base Salary** to the new amount.
- **Step 3:** Note that this change does not retroactively alter previously generated payslips; it only applies to future payroll runs.

---

> [!WARNING]  
> **Warning:** Be extremely cautious when modifying base salaries. Because this directly feeds into the Payroll module, a typo here (e.g., adding an extra zero) will result in massively inflated payslip generation!
