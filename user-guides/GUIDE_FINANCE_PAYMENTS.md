# 📘 Payments Guide\n\n> **Module:** Payments\n> **Section:** Finance & Accounting\n> **Access Levels:** Director, Admin, Finance Manager, Accountant\n\n---\n\n## 🌐 Overview\nRecord and track all incoming payments against student invoices.\n\n## ✨ Key Features\n- Record new payments\n- Link payments to specific invoices/students\n- Track payment methods\n\n---\n\n## 🚀 Step-by-Step Instructions\n\n### Recording a Payment\n\n1. Click **Payments** in the sidebar.
2. Click **+ Add Payment**.
3. Select the related **Invoice** or **Student**.
4. Enter the **Amount Paid**, **Payment Date**, and **Method** (e.g., Cash, Transfer).
5. Click **Save**.\n\n---\n*For further assistance, refer to the main User Guide or contact your System Administrator.*\n
