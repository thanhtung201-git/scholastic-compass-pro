# 📘 Payments Guide

> **Module:** Payments
> **Section:** Finance & Accounting
> **Access Levels:** Director, Admin, Finance Manager, Accountant

---

## 🌐 Overview
The **Payments** menu serves as a simplified, read-only transaction log. While the "Tuition" menu is used to actively generate invoices and record new money, the Payments menu provides a clean historical record of all completed incoming transactions.

## ✨ Key Features
- **Transaction Ledger:** A chronological list of every successful payment received.
- **Verification:** Quickly verify payment methods and amounts without opening individual student invoices.

---

## 🚀 How to Use (Step-by-Step)

### 1. Reviewing the Payment Ledger
- **Step 1:** Navigate to **Finance & Accounting > Payments**.
- **Step 2:** The dashboard presents a straightforward ledger with the following columns: **Date, Student, Invoice ID, Method, and Amount**.
- **Step 3:** Use this table at the end of the day to reconcile your cash drawer or bank statement. 
- **Step 4:** For example, if you sort the "Method" column by "Cash", the sum of the "Amount" column should exactly match the physical cash in your register for that specific Date.

---

> [!TIP]
> **Pro Tip:** If you notice an error in a payment listed here (e.g., recorded as Cash but it was actually a Bank Transfer), you must navigate back to the **Tuition** menu, locate the specific Invoice ID, and edit the payment record there!
