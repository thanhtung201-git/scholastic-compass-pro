# 📘 Balance Sheet Guide

> **Module:** Balance Sheet
> **Section:** Finance & Accounting
> **Access Levels:** Director, Admin, Finance Manager, Accountant

---

## 🌐 Overview
The **Balance Sheet** (Bảng Cân Đối Kế Toán) provides a high-level summary of your organization's financial health by aggregating all assets, liabilities, and equity over a specific time period.

## ✨ Key Features
- **Dynamic Filtering:** Filter the financial data by Month, Quarter, or Year to track seasonal changes.
- **Detailed Ledgers:** Review the exact line items that make up your total assets and liabilities.

---

## 🚀 How to Use (Step-by-Step)

### 1. Generating a Financial Report
- **Step 1:** Navigate to **Finance & Accounting > Balance Sheet**.
- **Step 2:** Locate the filtering form fields at the top of the page.
- **Step 3:** Use the dropdowns to configure the report:
  - **Loại kỳ (Period Type):** Choose whether you want to view by Month (Tháng), Quarter (Quý), or Year (Năm).
  - Select the specific Month/Quarter/Year you wish to analyze.
- **Step 4:** The data tables will automatically refresh.

### 2. Reading the Balance Sheet
- **Step 1:** The interface is split into two primary tables: **TÀI SẢN** (Assets) and **NGUỒN VỐN** (Liabilities/Equity).
- **Step 2:** Each table displays columns for **Mã số** (Code), **Chỉ tiêu** (Indicator), **Kỳ này** (Current Period), and **Kỳ trước** (Previous Period).
- **Step 3:** Compare the "Kỳ này" column against "Kỳ trước" to instantly identify whether your financial position has improved or declined compared to the last cycle.

---

> [!TIP]
> **Pro Tip:** Because the Balance Sheet aggregates data automatically, if you notice a discrepancy in the "Kỳ này" column, you should immediately check the **Expenses (Phiếu Thu/Chi)** menu to ensure all vouchers for the current month have been properly approved!
