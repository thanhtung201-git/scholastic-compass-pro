# 📘 Rooms Guide

> **Module:** Rooms
> **Section:** Academic
> **Access Levels:** Director, Admin, Academic Manager, Academic Staff

---

## 🌐 Overview
The **Rooms** menu is a simple but vital resource management tool. It allows you to inventory the physical classrooms across your campuses, ensuring that the scheduling system knows exactly what spaces are available and how many students they can hold.

## ✨ Key Features
- **Capacity Enforcement:** By setting room capacities, you prevent the "Classes" module from over-enrolling students into a space that is physically too small.
- **Resource Tracking:** Manage physical locations and their associated equipment.

---

## 🚀 How to Use (Step-by-Step)

### 1. Creating a New Room
- **Step 1:** Navigate to **Academic > Rooms**.
- **Step 2:** Click to open the **Add Room** dialog.
- **Step 3:** Enter the room specifics:
  - **Room Name:** E.g., "Room 101" or "Main Auditorium".
  - **Branch:** Assign the room to a specific physical campus.
  - **Capacity:** The absolute maximum number of students the room can safely hold.
- **Step 4:** Click **Save**.

### 2. Assigning Rooms to Classes
- **Step 1:** While the Rooms menu manages the *inventory*, you actually utilize these rooms in the **Classes** and **Schedule** menus.
- **Step 2:** When creating a new class or scheduled lesson, the dropdown for "Classroom" will dynamically populate with the active rooms you created here.

---

> [!TIP]
> **Pro Tip:** If a room undergoes renovation (e.g., new desks are added), remember to update its Capacity here immediately so the Academic Manager knows they can enroll more students!
