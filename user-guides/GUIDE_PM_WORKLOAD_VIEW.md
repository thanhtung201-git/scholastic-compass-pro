# 📘 Workload View Guide

> **Module:** Workload View
> **Section:** Project Management
> **Access Levels:** All Roles

---

## 🌐 Overview
The **Workload View** is a critical resource management tool. It aggregates data from the Task Assignment and Kanban Board menus to provide a highly visual summary of exactly how many tasks each team member is currently juggling, helping managers prevent employee burnout.

## ✨ Key Features
- **Capacity Visualization:** See a high-level summary of assigned tasks broken down by individual employee.
- **Burnout Prevention:** Instantly spot if one person has 15 active tasks while another only has 2.
- **Sprint Preparation:** Essential for reviewing team capacity before starting a new Sprint.

---

## 🚀 How to Use (Step-by-Step)

### 1. Analyzing Team Capacity
- **Step 1:** Navigate to **Project Management > Workload View**.
- **Step 2:** The dashboard presents a grid or chart mapping out your team members.
- **Step 3:** Next to each employee, you will see a count of their active, pending, and completed tasks.
- **Step 4:** Look for severe imbalances. If an employee's workload bar is glowing red or significantly longer than others, they are a bottleneck risk.

### 2. Rebalancing Work
- **Step 1:** Identify an overworked team member in the Workload View.
- **Step 2:** Navigate back to the **Kanban Board** or **Task Assignment** menus.
- **Step 3:** Filter for tasks assigned to that specific person.
- **Step 4:** Edit the tasks to reassign them to team members who currently have a lighter workload.

---

> [!WARNING]  
> **Warning:** Failing to check the Workload View before assigning massive new projects is the number one cause of missed deadlines. Always verify your team actually has the capacity to take on new work!
