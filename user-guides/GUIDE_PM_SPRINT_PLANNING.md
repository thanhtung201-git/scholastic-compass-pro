# 📘 Sprint Planning Guide

> **Module:** Sprint Planning
> **Section:** Project Management
> **Access Levels:** All Roles

---

## 🌐 Overview
The **Sprint Planning** menu is essential for agile teams. Instead of tackling a massive project all at once, teams break work down into short, focused periods called "Sprints" (typically 1 or 2 weeks). This module allows managers to group specific tasks into these time-boxed sprints.

## ✨ Key Features
- **Sprint Management:** Create specific timeframes with firm start and end dates.
- **Task Allocation:** Move tasks from a general backlog directly into an active sprint.
- **Focused Execution:** Keeps the team locked in on immediate goals rather than distant project deadlines.

---

## 🚀 How to Use (Step-by-Step)

### 1. Creating a New Sprint
- **Step 1:** Navigate to **Project Management > Sprint Planning**.
- **Step 2:** Click the button to open the **Create New Sprint** dialog.
- **Step 3:** Define the sprint boundaries:
  - **Name:** E.g., "Sprint 14: Mid-term Prep".
  - **Start Date:** The day the team begins the work.
  - **End Date:** The day the team must finish the work.
- **Step 4:** Save the sprint.

### 2. Assigning Tasks to the Sprint
- **Step 1:** Once your sprint is created, click the action to **Assign Task to Sprint**.
- **Step 2:** You will typically see a list of "Backlog" tasks (tasks that have been created but not yet scheduled).
- **Step 3:** Select the tasks you want the team to commit to completing during this specific sprint timeframe.
- **Step 4:** Once the tasks are moved into the sprint, the team uses the **Kanban Board** to execute them.

---

> [!TIP]
> **Pro Tip:** Never add more tasks to a sprint than your team can realistically complete! Check the **Workload View** first to gauge team capacity before assigning tasks to a new sprint.
