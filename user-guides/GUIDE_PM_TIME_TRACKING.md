# 📘 Time Tracking Guide

> **Module:** Time Tracking
> **Section:** Project Management
> **Access Levels:** All Roles

---

## 🌐 Overview
The **Time Tracking** menu allows staff to record exactly how many hours they spend on specific tasks. This data is critical for managers to assess team efficiency, accurately bill clients for project work, and improve future sprint planning estimates.

## ✨ Key Features
- **Manual Logging:** Easily backfill hours worked on previous days.
- **Task Integration:** Every time entry is linked directly to an existing project task.
- **Efficiency Audits:** Compare the estimated task duration against the actual time logged.

---

## 🚀 How to Use (Step-by-Step)

### 1. Adding a Manual Time Log
- **Step 1:** Navigate to **Project Management > Time Tracking**.
- **Step 2:** Click the button to open the **Add Manual Time Log** dialog.
- **Step 3:** Fill out the log details:
  - **Task:** Select the specific task you worked on from the dropdown list.
  - **Date:** The day the work occurred.
  - **Duration:** Enter the hours and minutes spent.
  - **Description:** Provide a brief summary of what you accomplished during that block of time.
- **Step 4:** Save the entry.

### 2. Reviewing Team Timesheets
- **Step 1:** Managers can view the main dashboard to see aggregated logs from the entire team.
- **Step 2:** Use the date and user filters to generate reports on how much total time was spent on a specific project last week, identifying where budget overruns might be occurring.

---

> [!TIP]
> **Pro Tip:** Make a habit of logging your time at the end of every single work day. Waiting until Friday afternoon to log your hours makes it incredibly difficult to remember exactly how long a specific task took on Monday morning!
