# 📘 Tuition Invoices Guide

> **Module:** Tuition
> **Section:** Finance & Accounting
> **Access Levels:** Director, Admin, Finance Manager, Accountant

---

## 🌐 Overview
The **Tuition** menu is where you manage the billing cycle for all enrolled students. It allows you to track outstanding balances, record partial or full payments, and view detailed invoice histories.

## ✨ Key Features
- **Invoice Tracking:** See at a glance which students owe money and which are fully paid.
- **Payment Recording:** Log partial payments or full settlements and note the payment method.
- **Receipt Details:** Open an invoice to view the exact payment notes and transaction history.

---

## 🚀 How to Use (Step-by-Step)

### 1. Reviewing Outstanding Invoices
- **Step 1:** Navigate to **Finance & Accounting > Tuition**.
- **Step 2:** The main table displays all active bills with columns for **Invoice, Student, Amount Due, Paid, Remaining, Due Date, Payment Note, and Status**.
- **Step 3:** Look for invoices where the "Status" is marked as "Unpaid" or "Partial".

### 2. Recording a Payment
- **Step 1:** Locate the student's unpaid invoice in the table.
- **Step 2:** Click the action to open the **Record Payment** dialog.
- **Step 3:** Fill out the payment details:
  - **Amount (VND):** Enter the exact amount the student just paid (whether it's the full remaining amount or a partial deposit).
  - **Payment Method:** Select how they paid (e.g., Cash, Bank Transfer, Card).
- **Step 4:** Click **Save** or submit. The system will automatically update the "Paid" and "Remaining" columns. If the remaining balance hits 0, the status automatically switches to "Paid".

### 3. Viewing Invoice Details
- **Step 1:** You can click to open the **Invoice Details** modal on any invoice to see the historical log of exactly when payments were made and what payment notes were attached.

---

> [!TIP]
> **Pro Tip:** When students are first added to a class in the "Students" menu, the system automatically generates an initial unpaid invoice for them here. You rarely need to create invoices manually!
