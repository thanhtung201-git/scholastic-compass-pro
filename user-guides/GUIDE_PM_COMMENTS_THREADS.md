# 📘 Comments & Threads Guide

> **Module:** Comments & Threads
> **Section:** Project Management
> **Access Levels:** All Roles

---

## 🌐 Overview
The **Comments & Threads** menu serves as the internal communication hub for your project management workflows. Instead of discussing task details in external chat applications where context is lost, team members can collaborate directly within the ERP.

## ✨ Key Features
- **Contextual Communication:** Messages are tied directly to specific projects or tasks.
- **Historical Record:** New team members can read past threads to instantly catch up on project decisions.
- **Centralized Inbox:** View a feed of all recent chatter across projects you are assigned to.

---

## 🚀 How to Use (Step-by-Step)

### 1. Participating in a Thread
- **Step 1:** Navigate to **Project Management > Comments & Threads**.
- **Step 2:** The dashboard displays a list of active conversation threads related to your tasks.
- **Step 3:** Click on a thread to expand the conversation history.
- **Step 4:** Use the text input field at the bottom of the screen to type your message or ask a question.
- **Step 5:** Click **Send** to post it. Your teammates will be able to see the update immediately.

### 2. Contextual Access
- **Step 1:** You do not always need to navigate to this specific menu to use the feature. 
- **Step 2:** When viewing a task inside the **Kanban Board** or **Task Assignment** menus, you can often open a slide-out panel or modal to view the specific comment thread attached to that exact task.
