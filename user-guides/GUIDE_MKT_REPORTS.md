# 📘 Marketing Reports Guide

> **Module:** Reports
> **Section:** Marketing
> **Access Levels:** Director, Admin, Marketing Manager

---

## 🌐 Overview
The **Reports** menu aggregates all raw data from your Leads, Campaigns, and Sources modules to generate actionable business intelligence. It provides Directors and Marketing Managers with visual charts that prove exactly how effective the advertising budget is.

## ✨ Key Features
- **Conversion Analytics:** Track the percentage of leads that successfully convert into paying students.
- **Source ROI:** Visually compare which marketing channels (e.g., Facebook vs. Google) bring in the most revenue.
- **Campaign Effectiveness:** Review the Cost-Per-Lead metrics for specific advertising campaigns.

---

## 🚀 How to Use (Step-by-Step)

### 1. Generating Performance Reports
- **Step 1:** Navigate to **Marketing > Reports**.
- **Step 2:** The dashboard will display a variety of pre-configured charts (e.g., Lead Conversion Funnel, Leads by Source).
- **Step 3:** Locate the date filter controls at the top of the page.
- **Step 4:** Select the timeframe you want to analyze (e.g., "Last 30 Days" or "Q1 2026"). The charts will dynamically recalculate.

### 2. Making Data-Driven Decisions
- **Step 1:** Look at the "Leads by Source" chart. If "Local Flyers" brought in 0 leads but cost $500, you know to cut that budget next month.
- **Step 2:** Look at the Conversion Funnel. If you have 500 "New" leads but only 10 "Enrolled" students, your sales team's follow-up process needs immediate improvement.

---

> [!TIP]
> **Pro Tip:** Marketing reports are only as accurate as the data your staff inputs! If the sales team fails to ask leads "Where did you hear about us?" and leaves the Source field blank, these reports will be useless.
